# Decision — DEC-001

**Status:** Signed-off
**Date:** Week 3
**Decision Owner:** Priya Nair
**Raised By:** Project Manager (while preparing WP-2.2)

## Context

The invitation needs a dietary form. Do we make every attendee fill it in, or only those who have
needs? This does not change scope, schedule, budget, or resources, so it is a Decision, not a
Change Request.

## Options Considered

| Option | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|
| Everyone fills in the form | Complete list; no assumption about who has needs | Slightly more effort for attendees |
| Only people with needs reply | Less effort for attendees | Silence is ambiguous, so risk R-003 grows |

## Decision

Everyone fills in the form, with a "no needs" tick-box, so every one of the 40 invitees has a
recorded answer. This also gives a clean completion measure for WP-3.1.

## Consequences

- WP-2.2's invitation text must include the mandatory form link.
- WP-3.1's acceptance criterion ("a reply from every one of the 40 invitees") is directly testable.
- R-003 stays Avoid, with a stronger plan.

## Amendments

None.
