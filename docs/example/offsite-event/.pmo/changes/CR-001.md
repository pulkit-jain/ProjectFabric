# Change Request — CR-001

## Description

Move the offsite from Week 8 to Week 9, and the wrap-up from Week 9 to Week 10.

## Reason / Trigger

Risk R-001 happened. Of the three shortlisted venues, only Lakeside Conference Centre meets the
room and catering requirements, and it is free in Week 9 but not in Week 8.

## Impact Analysis

| Dimension | Impact |
|---|---|
| Scope | None |
| Schedule | Milestones "Offsite held" and "Wrap-up sent" move one week later; the year-end budget freeze is in Week 12, so there is still a two-week buffer |
| Cost | None; the venue quote is the same for Week 9 |
| Resource | None; the team has the same capacity in Week 9 |
| Risk | R-001 is closed. Travel prices may rise slightly with one extra week of lead time, so R-002 stays open |
| Quality | None |
| Procurement | The venue contract (V-001) is written for the Week 9 dates |

## Options

| Option | Trade-off | Recommended |
|---|---|---|
| A | Move the event to Week 9 at Lakeside | Yes |
| B | Keep Week 8 at the second-choice venue, which has no breakout rooms | No: breaks the workshop design |
| C | Move to Week 11, after the freeze buffer shrinks to one week | No: too close to the freeze |

## Decision

| Approver | Decision | Date |
|---|---|---|
| Marta Silva | Approved (Option A) | Week 2 |

## Baseline Updates Required

- `schedule.md`: milestones "Offsite held" (Week 8 to Week 9) and "Wrap-up sent" (Week 9 to Week 10);
  work packages 4.1 and 4.2 moved by one week.
- `charter.md`: milestone summary updated to match.
