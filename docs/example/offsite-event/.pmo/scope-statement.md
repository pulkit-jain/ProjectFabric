# Scope Statement

## Project Scope Description

A two-day offsite for the 40-person Operations department. Day 1 is team-building and talks; day 2
is a facilitated workshop that ends in an agreed list of next-year priorities. The project covers
everything from booking the venue to sending the wrap-up note.

## Deliverables

| # | Deliverable | Acceptance Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Venue and catering | A signed booking for the event dates and a confirmed menu with every dietary need covered |
| 2 | Programme and content | A two-day agenda approved by the Event Lead, invitations sent, and a facilitator booked |
| 3 | Attendees and travel | An attendance list, plus travel and rooms booked for everyone who is attending |
| 4 | Event delivery and close | The offsite has run, feedback is collected, and a wrap-up note with the priority list is sent |

## Exclusions

- Partner or family attendance
- International travel and visas
- Branded merchandise

## Constraints

- Budget ceiling of about 18,000 EUR
- Event before the year-end budget freeze

## Assumptions

- The venue has two breakout rooms available both days
- Everyone has manager approval to attend
