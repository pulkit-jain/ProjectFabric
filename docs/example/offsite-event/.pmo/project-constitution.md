# Project Constitution

## Workflow Preset

**Preset:** agile-hybrid

## Overrides of team.md

| team.md Item | Team Value | This Project's Value | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Control cycle cadence | Weekly | Weekly, plus one extra cycle in the week before the event | Last-minute changes are most likely then |

## Principles

1. The event date is fixed once the venue is booked; scope flexes before the date does.
2. Nothing that costs money is committed without the Sponsor's sign-off.
3. Every attendee's dietary and access needs are recorded before catering is confirmed.

## Decision Authority

| Decision Type | Approver | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| Scope change | Marta Silva (Sponsor) | Any change to `wbs.md` |
| Schedule change | Marta Silva (Sponsor) | Any change to baseline milestone dates |
| Budget change | Marta Silva (Sponsor) | Variance beyond 10% of the charter budget |
| Risk acceptance | Priya Nair (Event Lead) | Score of 8 or below; above that, the Sponsor |

## Reporting Cadence

A status report every week (`/pf-control-cycle`), read by the Sponsor and the Event Lead.

## Escalation Rules

A deviation becomes a Change Request when it moves a milestone date, changes the headcount the
venue is booked for, or changes the budget by more than 10%. Anything smaller is absorbed and
noted in the tracker.

## Definition of Ready (project-level additions)

- Any work package that commits money has the Sponsor's sign-off recorded in its task.

## Definition of Done (project-level)

The offsite has run, feedback is collected, the wrap-up note is sent, and every vendor is paid
or has a closing note.
