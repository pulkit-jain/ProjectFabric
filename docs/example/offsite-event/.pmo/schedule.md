## Critical Path Notes

The longest chain is 1.1, 2.1, 2.2, 3.1, 3.2, then 4.1 and 4.2. Travel booking (3.2) cannot start
until attendance is known (3.1), which cannot start until invitations are out (2.2), so late
invitations push the travel work directly. Catering (1.2) and the facilitator (2.3) have about
three weeks of slack before the event.
