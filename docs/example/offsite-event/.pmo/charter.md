# Project Charter

## Project Name

Company Offsite Event

## Sponsor

Marta Silva, Head of Operations

## Purpose / Business Case

The Operations department has grown from 25 to 40 people in a year and has never met all in one
place. A two-day offsite is meant to build working relationships across the three sub-teams and
agree next year's priorities. It is planned now so the event lands before the year-end budget
freeze.

## Objectives

| # | Objective | Measure of Success |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Bring all 40 department members together for two days | At least 90% attendance |
| 2 | Agree next year's department priorities | A signed-off priority list at the end of day 2 |
| 3 | Stay within budget | Total cost at or below the budget ceiling |

## Success Criteria

- At least 36 of 40 people attend both days.
- The priority list is agreed and sent to attendees within one week of the event.
- Average attendee rating of 4 out of 5 or better in the feedback survey.

## High-Level Scope

**In scope:**
- Venue and catering booking
- Agenda, invitations, and facilitation for two days
- Attendance, dietary needs, travel, and rooms
- Running the event and collecting feedback

**Out of scope:**
- Partner or family attendance
- International travel and visas
- Branded merchandise

## High-Level Requirements

- Space for 40 people plus two breakout rooms
- Vegetarian and allergen-aware catering
- Venue reachable by train from the head office

## Assumptions

- Everyone can travel and has a manager's approval to attend.
- The budget ceiling includes catering, venue, travel, and the facilitator.

## Constraints

- Budget ceiling of about 18,000 EUR.
- The event must happen before the year-end budget freeze.
- Team of two part-time people plus an AI team member and one venue vendor.

## High-Level Risks

- The preferred venue may not be free in the target week.
- Attendees may respond late, delaying travel booking.

## Milestone Summary

| Milestone | Target |
|---|---|
| Venue booked | Week 2 |
| Invitations sent | Week 5 |
| Travel and rooms confirmed | Week 8 |
| Offsite held | Week 9 (revised from Week 8 by CR-001) |
| Wrap-up sent | Week 10 |

## Budget Summary (order of magnitude)

About 18,000 EUR, mostly venue and catering.
